## Scope

Home page (`src/app/page.tsx`), single long page with anchored chapters. Visitor mode: Experience, with a Persuade close (recruiter or client starts a conversation).

## Audience and job

Recruiters screening AI / ML engineer candidates (priority) and freelance clients. Job: in under a minute, know what Syed builds, believe it is real, and reach him. Proof: resume facts in PRODUCT.md only. Constraint: employer work at capability level.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio read like a well-edited feature about an engineer, not a resume grid. One person spanning model, data, and product, told in numbered chapters. Refuses the category default of a centred avatar, a skills-icon wall, and equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#030303 / #0b0b0b surfaces), warm off-white ink (#f1eee8), muted stone (#aaa9a4), one warm orange accent (#ff8c59) used for live signals and the italic emphasis only. Hairline rules at low ink opacity. Display: Instrument Serif at very large sizes with one italic word per headline. Body: DM Sans. Small tracked mono kickers ("02 / SELECTED WORK"). Pill links whose label rolls on hover. A live retrieval-graph canvas: nodes and edges drawn in ink and orange.

STORY: Visitor learns who (Syed Hammad, AI Engineer, Bengaluru), sees proof (NL2SQL, Graph RAG, enterprise AI, real metrics), understands range (model, data, ship), follows the journey 2019 to now, and emails or opens LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: name mark left, chapter links center-right, "Let's talk" pill and motion toggle right. Kicker line "AI ENGINEER. DATA SCIENTIST. BUILDER. / BASED IN BENGALURU". Giant serif "Syed Hammad" with "Hammad" italic, spanning most of the width, lower-left weighted. One-sentence thesis beneath, two rolling CTAs (primary: Explore my work; secondary: Start a conversation). The retrieval-graph canvas fills the right and background, quietly animating. Proof strip at the viewport bottom edge with real numbers.

FORM: Pinned by the user: the reference portfolio's editorial chaptered dark grammar (position 1 of 1, brief-pinned). Seed key: pinned-reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
