# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers screening candidates for AI / ML engineer roles. They arrive from LinkedIn, a resume link, or a job application, skim for under a minute, and need to answer "does this person build real LLM systems, and are they worth a call?".

Secondary: freelance or consulting clients looking for someone who can take an AI or web idea from data to a shipped product. Hiring is the priority when the two conflict.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Syed Hammad, an AI Engineer based in Bengaluru. It turns a resume into proof: what he builds (enterprise LLM applications, NL2SQL, Graph RAG, forecasting, full stack web), how he works, and how to reach him. Success is a recruiter or client starting a conversation (email, LinkedIn) after one visit.

## Positioning

An AI engineer who covers the whole path, not one slice of it: data science and ML foundations (MSc Big Data Analytics), LLM systems on enterprise data (NL2SQL, Graph RAG at NuMinds AI, enterprise AI at Eurofins), and five years of shipping client websites. Model, data, and product in one person.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare this site against other candidate portfolios, GitHub profiles, and LinkedIn. It is read on desktop during screening and on phones from LinkedIn links. It must load fast on Vercel and work without accounts or forms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Next.js 16 site (App Router) on Vercel, no backend. Contact is via mailto and external profiles.
- Content lives as typed data in `src/content/`.
- Work at current and past employers (Eurofins, NuMinds AI, Deepwoods) is described at capability level only; no confidential client names, datasets, or internal metrics.
- Undecided: public email (resume `syedhammad@myyahoo.com` vs old-site Gmail), portrait photo, whether a resume PDF is linked, final domain.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim ties to a role, a project, or a number from the resume.
2. Hiring first: a recruiter should know role, specialty, and how to reach him within one viewport.
3. Whole path: always show model, data, and product together; that range is the differentiator.
4. Discretion: employer work is described by capability, never by confidential detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
